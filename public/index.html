How it works

  1. Connect Spotify. We read your liked songs, followed artists, and top artists. Nothing else.
  2. Get your taste profile. A Taste Score, your top genres, and the hidden gems buried in your library.
  3. Start your ledger. Every obscure artist gets a timestamp the moment we first spot them. That timestamp is your proof you heard them first.
  4. Get milestone alerts. When a ledger artist's audience crosses 10K, 50K, 100K, 500K, or a million followers, Radar Pro members get pinged.
  5. Share the proof. Generate 1080×1350 cards and post your early calls anywhere.

What you get

Taste Score

A 0–100 score built from three plain ingredients: how much of your library sits below 40 popularity, how many distinct genres you span, and how deep your artist catalog runs. Illustrative — not a ranking, not a percentile.

Discovery ledger

A permanent, timestamped record of every under-the-radar artist in your library. New finds are added on every scan. "Heard it first" claims always point at real ledger timestamps — never invented history.

Milestone alerts

We re-check your ledger artists' follower counts and alert you when they cross audience thresholds. Based on Spotify follower counts — the Spotify API doesn't provide monthly listeners, and we don't pretend it does.
